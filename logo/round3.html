Keypool: logo concepts, round 3

Seven more ideas, O to U. Earlier rounds: A to F and G to N. Pick one and the whole site is built from it.

O Upright key

keypool
app icon / tab / favicon

A key standing straight up, with a ring on top and teeth down one side. Reads as a numeral 1 and as a key.

Site built around it: Tomato red with near-black ink. Bold, poster-like, one big key per screen.

P Slices

keypool
app icon / tab / favicon

A circle cut into slabs that have slid out of line, with one slab picked out. A pool of separate pieces that still make one whole.

Site built around it: Black with white and one turquoise slab. Sliding, sharp-edged motion.

Q Cube

keypool
app icon / tab / favicon

A solid cube with a keyhole in one face. A sealed box with one way in.

Site built around it: Mint-grey with deep green. Tidy isometric diagrams, soft blocky shadows.

R kp

keypool
app icon / tab / favicon

The letters k and p built from plain strokes into one compact monogram that fits any square.

Site built around it: Indigo with sunny yellow. Chunky letterforms, big type, playful but tidy.

S One-eyed key

keypool
app icon / tab / favicon

A friendly key character with a single eye. One eye, one secret. It gives the brand a face and a mascot.

Site built around it: Bubble-gum pink and plum. Playful, rounded, with the mascot reacting to what you do.

T Orbit

keypool
app icon / tab / favicon

A single ring with one bright bead riding it. One thing in motion, in a closed loop.

Site built around it: Midnight indigo with white and warm gold. Smooth orbiting motion, a calm sense of space.

U Doorway

keypool
app icon / tab / favicon

An arched doorway with a single dot, like a keyhole at the end of a corridor. One way in, and you hold it.

Site built around it: Rust red with warm white. Arched shapes, architectural layout, big quiet spaces.
